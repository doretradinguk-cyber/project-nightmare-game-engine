# The Laughing Jester — Original Entity Specification

## Identity

The Jester is Project Nightmare's signature recurring entity.

It is not a conventional clown or borrowed horror character. Its visual identity is built from corrupted pixels, angular costume geometry, a split red/green signal palette and an unnatural fixed smile.

## Manifestation Sequence

### 01 — Rain
Glyphs and numerical symbols fall across the environment.

### 02 — Pattern
A few glyphs begin repeating in a recognisable arrangement.

### 03 — Face
Eyes and an impossible smile briefly appear inside the rain.

### 04 — Silhouette
The complete figure assembles from the digital rain.

### 05 — Displacement
The image disappears and may reappear somewhere physically impossible.

### 06 — Physical Manifestation
The player eventually encounters the Jester as a real environmental entity.

## Behaviour

- watches rather than constantly attacks
- appears at distance
- can disappear when directly approached
- can be detected through surveillance equipment
- may alter environmental systems
- uses laughter as a directional clue
- should remain unpredictable

## Visual Constraints

- pixel-inspired geometry
- original silhouette
- no recognisable existing character costume
- restrained red/green glow
- dark neutral materials
- expression readable even at low resolution
